# DeliverIQ Build 6 — Delivery Intelligence & Predictive Insights

Build 6 adds trend and change intelligence on top of the Build 5 AI Command Centre.

## New capabilities
- Delivery snapshots for historical comparison
- Risk and issue trend tracking
- RAID ageing signals
- Delivery confidence score
- Change-since-last-review briefing
- Predictive watchlist (advisory, not deterministic)
- Executive trend summary
- Server-side AI intelligence endpoint using real project data

## Principle
DeliverIQ should not claim to predict the future with certainty.
It surfaces risk signals, deterioration patterns and likely delivery pressure based on observed project data.

## Main user experience
A project leader opens the Command Centre and sees:

- What changed since last review
- What is getting worse
- What is improving
- What needs immediate attention
- Delivery confidence
- Recommended next actions

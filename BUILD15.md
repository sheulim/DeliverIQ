# DeliverIQ Build 15 — Financial & Budget Intelligence

Build 15 adds project financial governance, budget tracking, forecast-at-completion, variance analysis and value-for-money insight.

## New capabilities
- Project budget register
- Cost categories
- Actual spend tracking
- Commitments / accruals
- ETC and EAC
- Budget variance
- Burn rate
- Cost forecast confidence
- Benefits-vs-cost view
- AI financial review
- "Are we likely to exceed budget?"
- Programme-level financial roll-up
- Financial review history

## Product principle
Delivery health is incomplete without financial health.
DeliverIQ should help leaders understand not just whether work is progressing, but whether the cost profile remains credible.

## Important limitation
Forecasts are advisory. Where cost data is incomplete, DeliverIQ must explicitly flag uncertainty.

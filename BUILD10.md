# DeliverIQ Build 10 — Action & Decision Intelligence

Build 10 adds an accountability layer across projects and programmes.

## New capabilities
- Action register
- Decision register
- Owners and due dates
- Overdue action detection
- Action ageing
- Decision status and rationale
- AI commitment review
- "What commitments are slipping?"
- Turn AI recommendations into trackable actions
- Programme-level action roll-up
- Action / decision history

## Product principle
DeliverIQ should not stop at identifying problems.
It should help ensure agreed actions, decisions and escalations are followed through.

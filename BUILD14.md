# DeliverIQ Build 14 — Resource, Capacity & Delivery Forecast Intelligence

Build 14 adds resource planning, team capacity, throughput trends, and evidence-based forecast intelligence.

## New capabilities
- Resource / team register
- Capacity allocation
- Demand vs available capacity
- Sprint / period throughput history
- Utilisation and bottleneck signals
- Capacity risk scoring
- Delivery forecast review
- "Can we realistically deliver this scope by the target date?"
- Programme-level capacity roll-up
- Forecast history

## Product principle
A target date is not credible unless scope, capacity, throughput, dependencies and delivery risks support it.
DeliverIQ should help PMs challenge unrealistic plans with evidence rather than intuition alone.

## Important limitation
This build provides advisory forecast intelligence, not a guaranteed delivery prediction.
Where complete schedule and effort data are missing, the AI must explicitly state uncertainty.

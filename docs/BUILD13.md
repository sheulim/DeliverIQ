# DeliverIQ Build 13 — Benefits, OKR & Value Realisation Intelligence

Build 13 connects delivery execution to measurable business outcomes.

## New capabilities
- Benefits register
- OKR tracking
- KPI / outcome tracking
- Planned vs realised value
- Benefit owners and target dates
- Benefit confidence and status
- AI value realisation review
- "Are we delivering the intended business value?"
- Programme-level benefit roll-up
- Outcome history

## Product principle
A project can be on time and still fail to create business value.
DeliverIQ should help leaders understand both delivery health and outcome realisation.

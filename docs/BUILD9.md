# DeliverIQ Build 9 — Dependency Intelligence & Impact Simulation

Build 9 adds delivery decision support for dependencies, milestones and schedule pressure.

## New capabilities
- Dependency intelligence workspace
- Cross-project dependency mapping
- Dependency criticality scoring
- Downstream milestone linkage
- Schedule slip simulation
- "What happens if this slips?" AI analysis
- Critical-path style watchlist
- Impact radius across projects
- Suggested mitigation and escalation actions
- Simulation history

## Important product principle
DeliverIQ does not claim to calculate a mathematically exact critical path unless the project contains complete activity durations and predecessor relationships.
Instead, Build 9 provides a delivery-criticality model based on:
- dependency status
- due dates
- downstream links
- milestone exposure
- project health
- explicit impact fields
- known cross-project relationships

AI simulation results are advisory and should be reviewed by the project/programme lead.

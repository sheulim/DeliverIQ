# DeliverIQ Build 11 — Meeting Intelligence & Governance Copilot

Build 11 adds meeting-to-action governance workflows.

## New capabilities
- Meeting register
- Meeting notes capture
- AI-generated minutes
- Extracted actions and decisions
- Link extracted items to project Actions / Decisions
- Governance meeting types
- Follow-up tracking
- Meeting history
- "What changed from this meeting?"
- Human review before creating records

## Supported meeting types
- Steering Committee
- RAID Review
- Weekly Delivery Review
- Daily / Team Stand-up
- Programme Review
- Risk Review
- Custom

## Product principle
Meeting notes should not disappear into documents.
DeliverIQ converts them into structured governance records and follow-through.
